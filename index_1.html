How to read this

Below 50 means fear dominates — investors are nervous. Above 50 means confidence is running high. At extremes, markets often reverse: fear can signal a buying opportunity, greed can signal overheating.

How it works

Step 01 Headlines collected

Every morning the engine pulls 50+ financial headlines from Economic Times, Business Standard, and Moneycontrol — the most-read financial publications among Indian retail investors.

Step 02 AI reads the language

Each headline is scored by FinBERT — a transformer model trained specifically on financial text. It classifies each headline as positive, negative, or neutral with a confidence score.

Step 03 One score, one signal

Scores are averaged into a single daily number from 0–100. That number is stored and matched against the Nifty 50's next-day return to test whether sentiment predicts market movement.

The research

Research question

Does how the market feels today predict what it does tomorrow?

This tool was built to answer one question: does collective retail investor sentiment in India have predictive power over the Nifty 50's next-day returns?

Daily sentiment scores are logged and matched against actual market returns. A correlation analysis will be published once 60 trading days of data has been collected.
